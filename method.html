Method

How we uncover your story

1. Discovery

Find the story only you can tell.

What we do: We interview your employees, explore your internal tools and research the market to understand what makes you different, why it matters and why customers should care. We bring those insights together into a clear, memorable story.

What you get: A distinctive story that makes people lean in. A foundation for everything you say and do.

2. Alignment

Get everyone telling the same story.

What we do: We stretch your story for different audiences, from customers and employees to investors and partners. We work with your internal GTM teams to make the story relevant to their function, and help them tell it in their own words.

What you get: A shared understanding of what makes your company different, with every team telling the same story in their own words.

3. Application

Put your story to work across your business.

What we do: We build your story into sales decks, website pages, content plans and AI context layers. So it doesn’t just shape what you say, but how you communicate, make decisions and build your business.

What you get: A story that goes beyond a document to shape how your business communicates and operates.

4. Distribution (add-on)

Take your story to the world.

What we do: We help you take your story to market. From product launches to thought leadership, we develop the ideas and content that set you apart from the competition.

What you get: A distinctive voice in the market, and a story worth remembering.
